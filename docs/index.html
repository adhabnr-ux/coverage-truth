      Schools no one can check

      Carriers tell the FCC these schools have 4G, but in twelve months not one Ookla speed test was recorded within 2 km. Nothing in public crowdsourced data can confirm or contradict the claim.

      How to read this

      The claim

      FCC Broadband Data Collection, 4G LTE, all carriers, map of Dec 31 2025 (released Sept 2026). A school counts as claimed if its H3 hexagon (about 0.1 km²) is claimed at the 5/1 Mbps standard.

      The evidence

      Ookla open speed tests from Jul 2025 to Jun 2026, pooled across four quarters, within 2 km of the school. "Checked" means 10 or more tests. "Thin" means 1 to 9. "No one checked" means zero. Where 10 or more tests exist, every school averages above 5 Mbps download.

      The physics

      Around Black Mesa and Whiteriver, each carrier's weak-signal claims were tested against 30 m terrain with an ITU-R P.526 diffraction model, cross-checked with NVIDIA Sionna RT. Towers are either FCC-registered or located from the carriers' own strongest claims. Other areas are not yet modelled.

      What it does not show

      Speed tests are opt-in and cluster along roads, so a lack of tests is a verification gap, not proof of no service. Physics flags are predictions to check in the field, not findings of misconduct.
